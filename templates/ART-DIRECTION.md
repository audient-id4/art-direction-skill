## Artifact

The physical object, document or working thing this design steals its logic
from, and which of its properties transfer.

> 

## 1. Concept

The visual language in one sentence.

> 

## 2. Emotion

What someone should feel in the first two seconds.

> 

## 3. Recognition

With the logo cropped out of a screenshot, what identifies this?

> 

## 4. Metaphor

How the artifact's logic maps onto interface parts.

> 

## 5. Typography

Faces, and what each is for. Sizes, leading, tracking, measure. Why these
and not the neutral default.

> 

## 6. Geometry

The radius system, each value with its role. Where corners go sharp and why.
Nested-radius rule for this design.

> 

## 7. Spacing rhythm

The scale and where it came from. Where the composition compresses, where it
opens.

> 

## 8. Colour

Hierarchy, semantics, temperature, contrast strategy. The signature hue, if
there is one. Gradients: none, or exactly one and what it is doing.

> 

## 9. Interaction language

How this interface answers being touched. Entrance, hover, press, transition,
state change. Which are springs, which are eased, and why.

> 

## Signature detail

The one unexpected thing — and the work it does. A detail that only surprises
does not qualify.
